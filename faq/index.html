Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Rolex Vault, our collections, and the luxury watches we showcase.

What is Rolex Vault?

Rolex Vault is a premium showcase platform for luxury Rolex watches, featuring curated collections, specifications, and estimated prices.

Are the prices real?

Prices are estimated based on USD values converted into PHP using the exchange service inside the system.

Can I view all Rolex variants?

Yes, the Gallery page includes 8 Rolex variants including Datejust, GMT, Explorer, and Submariner models.

Do I need to log in?

Yes, Collection and Gallery pages are protected and require login first.
