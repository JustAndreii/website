The History of Rolex

Founded in 1905, Rolex has become one of the most prestigious luxury watch brands in the world. Known for its precision, durability, and timeless design, Rolex continues to set the standard in horology.

From the first waterproof watch, the Oyster, to modern innovations, Rolex represents excellence and craftsmanship.
